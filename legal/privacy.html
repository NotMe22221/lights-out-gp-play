2. What the Game stores on your device

The Game saves its data in your browser's local storage, on the device you play on:

  • your progress: credits, level, items, upgrades, career, championship, records and lap times;
  • your settings: graphics, sound, controls, driving assists and gameplay options;
  • the name, 3-letter code and car number you pick for your in-game driver;
  • custom circuits you build and your best-lap "ghost" recordings;
  • an age range (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) from the one question asked when the Game first starts. Your birth month and year are only used to work out that range; they are never stored or sent anywhere.

This storage is needed for the Game to work, so it does not need your consent. None of it is sent to us. To delete it, use Settings → Privacy & legal → Erase all data in the Game, or clear this site's data in your browser. A save file you export is yours to keep or delete.

3. Online races

Single-player and split-screen races do not use the race server (sponsored content is covered in section 6). When you create or join an online room, the Game connects to our race server. While you are connected, the server receives the following and passes it on to the other players in your room:

  • the room code and the room's race settings;
  • your 3-letter driver code, car number, team and livery colours;
  • your car's position, speed and lap times while you race;
  • the quick-chat messages you pick from the preset list;
  • your IP address and connection details, which the server needs to send data back to you and to limit connections per address to prevent abuse. Other players never see your IP address.

5. Children

The Game is suitable for players of all ages. When it first starts it asks for your birth month and year, with no answer preferred, and keeps only the age range on your device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age that applies in your country):

  • no accounts, real names or contact details are asked for;
  • online play uses 3-letter driver codes and preset quick-chat only, so there is no way to type and share personal details;
  • players under 13, and anyone who has not answered the age question, see sponsored content only in its contextual form, with no links (see section 6), and never get personalised ads, analytics identifiers or purchases.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

6. Sponsors and advertisers

Menus and circuits can show sponsored cards and trackside boards from brands that book them with us. Each one is labelled as sponsored, and we check and approve every brand before it appears.

  • Where it comes from: approved brands supply their logo, name and tagline, and our own server delivers them; the Game never connects to the brands or to an ad network. There are no third-party scripts, tracking pixels or cookies, and sponsors never receive information about individual players.
  • What the Game sends: only counts of how often each sponsor was shown or clicked, with no identifiers. Our server adds them up into daily totals for each sponsor, and the sponsor can see those totals.
  • Your IP address: when the Game loads the sponsors or sends those counts, our server uses your IP address, in its memory only, to stop abuse: to limit how many requests one address can make (it forgets the address within minutes of the last request) and, as a salted hash that cannot be turned back into the address, to limit how much one address can add to a sponsor's counts until the end of the day (UTC). It is never written to disk.
  • Children: players under 13, and anyone who has not answered the age question, see contextual sponsor content only: a logo, a name and a short tagline, with no links. Nothing about you is used to choose what is shown.
  • Sponsor websites: players aged 13 and over can open a sponsor's website from its card. That website is not ours, and its own privacy policy applies there.

If you book a sponsorship

Businesses book sponsorships on our advertiser pages. We use the contact name, email address, company, website, logo and booking details you give us, and the record of the advertiser terms you accepted, to review, run, bill and report on your booking and to contact you about it (performance of a contract, and our legal duty to keep accounts).

How long we keep it: our server deletes advertiser data on a fixed schedule:

  • a booking whose checkout was not completed: your contact details and logo are deleted 7 days later;
  • a booking we did not approve, or whose card authorisation expired before we reviewed it: your contact details and logo are deleted 30 days after that;
  • a booking that was paid: your logo is deleted 30 days after the sponsorship ends (or is cancelled). The billing records (the booking details, your contact details, the amounts and Stripe's reference numbers) are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 6 years for accounting] and then deleted.

Daily impression totals are kept for 13 months. While you are on Stripe's checkout page, the booking form, including your contact details and logo, is kept in your own browser tab (session storage) so that it can be filled in again if you cancel; it is deleted when you come back from checkout or close the tab.

Card payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure payment page, under Stripe's privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). We pass your email address to Stripe, which fills it in on the payment page and sends your receipt there, and the payment description names your brand. We never see or store card numbers; we keep Stripe's reference numbers for each payment, its amount and its status.
